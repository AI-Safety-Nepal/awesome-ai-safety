## Technical AI Safety

### Courses
- [Bluedot Technical AI Safety Course](https://bluedot.org/courses/technical-ai-safety)
- [Global Challenges Library - AI Alignment](https://globalchallengesproject.notion.site/AI-Alignment-59fa401974f94c629f3df23be88cdbcb)

### Fellowships

| Fellowship | Duration | Format | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| [MATS](https://www.matsprogram.org/) | 10 weeks + optional 6–12 month extension | In-person (Berkeley & London) | Flagship mentored research program pairing scholars with leading alignment researchers at Anthropic, Redwood, ARC, and UK AISI. Runs summer and autumn cohorts. 80% of alumni go on to work in alignment. |
| [MARS – Cambridge](https://caish.org/mars) | ~3 months | Hybrid (Cambridge kick-off + remote) | Run by Cambridge AI Safety Hub. Intensive in-person sprint week followed by 8–10 weeks of part-time remote research. Covers technical safety and governance. Compute budget, travel, accommodation, and meals covered. |
| [MARS – London](https://www.safeailondon.org/mars) | 2–3 months | Hybrid (London kick-off + remote) | Run by Safe AI London in collaboration with Cambridge AI Safety Hub. Covers mechanistic interpretability, AI governance, evals, and automated alignment research. |
| [LASR Labs](https://www.lasrlabs.org/) | 13 weeks | Full-time, in-person (London) | Teams of 3–4 work with supervisors from Google DeepMind, UK AISI, and top UK universities to write and submit an academic paper. Runs twice a year. Stipend plus food, office space, and travel. |
| [ERA Fellowship](https://erafellowship.org/) | 10 weeks | In-person (Cambridge, UK) | Fully funded residential fellowship for technical safety, AI philosophy, and governance research. Open to any career stage. Competitive stipend, accommodation, meals, transport, and visa support all covered. |
| [Anthropic Fellows Program](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/anthropic-fellows-program-2026/) | 4 months | Full-time (remote/hybrid) | Funded fellowship embedded at Anthropic working on scalable oversight, interpretability, AI control, and model welfare. No PhD or prior ML experience required. Over 80% of first cohort produced papers. |
| [Astra Fellowship](https://constellation.org/programs/astra) | 3–6 months | Full-time, in-person (Berkeley, CA) | Constellation's flagship fellowship advancing frontier AI safety projects with expert mentors and dedicated research management support. Monthly stipend. Fully funded. |
| [OpenAI Safety Fellowship](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-safety-fellowship/) | ~5 months | In-person (Berkeley) or remote | External research fellowship at OpenAI covering safety evaluation, robustness, agentic oversight, and misuse mitigation. Workspace available at Constellation alongside other fellows. |
| [Pivotal Research Fellowship](https://www.pivotal-research.org/fellowship) | 2 months | Remote-friendly | Independent research fellowship with mentorship from senior AI safety researchers. Open to ML, philosophy, policy, physics, and biology backgrounds. Past fellows have gone to UK AISI, GovAI, and Google DeepMind. |
| [PIBBSS Fellowship](https://princint.ai/programs/fellowship/) | ~3 months (incl. Prague residency) | Hybrid (remote + Prague) | For PhD and postdoctoral researchers from diverse fields (game theory, biology, economics, political science) bridging into AI safety. Monthly stipend plus travel. Dedicated tracks for Cooperative AI and Gradual Disempowerment. |
| [CAIS AI and Society Fellowship](https://safe.ai/fellowship) | 3 months | Full-time, in-person (San Francisco) | For social scientists and humanists (economics, law, international relations, philosophy) pivoting research toward AI. Fellows define their own research directions with significant autonomy. Fully funded. |
| [Global AI Safety Fellowship](https://globalaisafetyfellowship.com/) | Up to 6 months | Full-time, placement-based | Places exceptional STEM talent with leading AI safety organizations globally. Open to candidates from underrepresented regions. Includes a technical bootcamp for those new to the field. |
| [Algoverse AI Safety Fellowship](https://algoverseairesearch.org/ai-safety-fellowship) | 12 weeks | Part-time, remote | Teams of three work with expert mentors on alignment, interpretability, and robustness research. Access to compute and conference funding. Funded by Open Philanthropy. |
| [ARENA](https://www.arena.education/) | 4–5 weeks | In-person (London) | Intensive upskilling program for engineers entering technical AI safety research. Covers transformers, RL, and interpretability from first principles. Travel, accommodation, and meals covered. |
| [SPAR](https://sparai.org/) | 3 months | Part-time, fully remote | Pairs aspiring researchers with professional mentors on AI safety and policy projects. Commit 5–40 hrs/week alongside other commitments. Covers technical safety, AI policy, and biosecurity. Culminates in a Demo Day career fair. |
| [Future Impact Group (FIG) Fellowship](https://futureimpact.group/) | 12 weeks | Part-time, fully remote | Research associate roles on projects in AI governance, technical AI safety, and AI philosophy. Requires 8+ hrs/week with mentorship from experienced project leads. Runs winter and summer cohorts. |

## AI Governance

### Courses and Resource Collections
- [Bluedot Frontier AI Governance Course](https://bluedot.org/courses/ai-governance)
- [Global Challenges Library - AI Governance](https://globalchallengesproject.notion.site/AI-Governance-ba88139d9cfe41feb58b3edf3c1d786c)

### Fellowships

| Fellowship | Duration | Format | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| [GovAI Summer Fellowship – Research Track](https://www.governance.ai/) | 3 months | In-person (London) | Fellows at the Centre for the Governance of AI design an independent governance research project with expert mentorship. Open to early-career researchers from political science, law, economics, and CS. Stipend plus travel support. |
| [GovAI Summer Fellowship – Applied Track](https://www.governance.ai/) | 3 months | In-person (London) | For non-research careers in AI governance. Past projects include policy memos, communications strategy for AI safety orgs, and event organization. Paired with a GovAI mentor. Stipend support. |
| [GovAI Research Fellow](https://www.governance.ai/) | 2 years (renewable) | Full-time (London, hybrid options) | Senior research position covering risk, regulation, technical governance, international cooperation, and AI economics. For experienced researchers. |
| [IAPS AI Policy Fellowship](https://www.iaps.ai/fellowship) | 3 months | Remote-first (2-week D.C. residency required) | Run by the Institute for AI Policy and Strategy. Open worldwide to technical and policy backgrounds at all career stages. Alumni have gone to RAND, Institute for Progress, and government. |
| [Horizon Fellowship](https://horizonpublicservice.org/) | ~1 year | In-person (Washington, D.C.) | Places fellows at US government agencies, congressional offices, and think tanks to work on AI and emerging tech policy.  US work authorization required. |
| [TechCongress Congressional Innovation Fellowship](https://techcongress.io/) | 1 year | In-person (Washington, D.C.) | Places engineers and technologists directly in congressional offices and committees to inform live AI and technology legislation. |
| [Arcadia Impact AI Governance Taskforce](https://www.arcadiaimpact.org/ai-governance-taskforce) | 12 weeks | Part-time, fully remote | Multidisciplinary teams of four conduct AI governance research with expert partners from AISI, GovAI, and CLTR. 8 hrs/week. Runs autumn and winter cohorts. Designed to be compatible with existing employment. |
| [Tarbell Fellowship](https://www.tarbellfellowship.org/) | 9 months | In-person (host newsroom) | Places early-career journalists at major newsrooms (Wired, Washington Post, etc.) to cover AI risk and tech policy in the public interest. Includes a 3-month intensive study period and cohort summit. |
