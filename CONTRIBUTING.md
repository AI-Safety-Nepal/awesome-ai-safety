# Contributing to Awesome AI Safety

Thank you for helping keep this list useful and up to date.

## How to Contribute

1. Fork the repository and create a new branch.
2. Make your changes following the guidelines below.
3. Open a pull request with a brief description of what you're adding or changing.

## What We're Looking For

**New fellowships, courses or opportunities**
- Must be recurring (not a one-time program)
- Must be genuinely focused on AI safety, alignment, or AI governance

**Corrections and updates**
- Deadlines, URLs, and program details change frequently, fixes are very welcome
- If a program has ended or is no longer recurring, feel free to flag it for removal

## What We're Not Looking For

- One-off events, conferences, or workshops
- Programs that are not focused on safety (general ML research programs, unless they have a dedicated safety track)
- Paid courses or resources behind a significant paywall
